How it works

Four simple steps every morning. You stay in control of your calendar.

STEP 1

Read

Each morning it checks your Gmail (read-only) for new emails from the senders you track.

STEP 2

Find

An AI model picks out each event, and a second check verifies it against the original email.

STEP 3

Ask

You get a Telegram message with each event and two buttons: Add or Skip.

STEP 4

Add

Only when you tap Add does it go on your Google Calendar. Changed your mind? Undo with one tap.

Frequently asked questions

Does it read all of my emails?

It only looks for events in emails from the senders you choose to track, such as your children's school.

Will it add things to my calendar on its own?

No. Every event waits for your approval on Telegram. Nothing is added until you tap Add.

What if it gets a date wrong?

Each event is checked a second time against the original email, and you see it before it is added. If something slips through, tap Undo to remove it.

Why Telegram?

Telegram lets the bot show buttons right inside the message, so approving an event takes one tap.

How do I get access?

Schedule Bot is in private testing with a limited number of users. Email us to request access.
