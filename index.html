Schedule Bot

Schedule Bot finds dates hidden in your emails and helps you put them on your calendar, but only after you say yes.

Parents get a constant stream of school notices, club sign-ups and event reminders. The dates are easy to miss. Schedule Bot reads the emails you choose to track, picks out the events, and sends you a short daily summary on Telegram.

How it works

  1. Read: each morning it checks your Gmail (read-only) for new emails from the senders you track.
  2. Find: an AI model picks out the events (date, time, title), and a second check verifies each one against the original email.
  3. Ask: you get a Telegram message with each event and two buttons: Add or Skip.
  4. Add: only when you tap Add does it write the event to your Google Calendar. You can undo it with one tap.

What it will never do

  • Send, delete or change your emails. Its Gmail access is read-only.
  • Add anything to your calendar without your tap.
  • Use your data for advertising, or sell it to anyone.

Status

Schedule Bot is a small, independent project in private testing with a limited number of users.
