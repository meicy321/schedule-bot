Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what data Schedule Bot accesses, why, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. Schedule Bot is run by an independent developer. Contact: nanoclawmi@gmail.com.

1. Data we access

SourceAccessWhy
GmailRead-only (gmail.readonly)To find event dates in emails from the senders you choose to track.
Google CalendarCreate, edit and delete events (calendar.events)To add an event after you approve it, to avoid duplicates, and to undo an event when you ask.
Google DriveRead-only (drive.readonly)To read Google Docs or Sheets that are linked inside a tracked email (for example, a school sign-up sheet).
TelegramMessages you send to the botTo send you summaries and receive your choices (Add / Skip / Undo), and to read text or screenshots you forward to the bot.

Optional sources, only if you turn them on: TimeTree (to check your existing family calendar) and Notion (only pages you share with the integration).

2. What we store

  • We do not store your emails. Email content is read, used to find events, and then discarded.
  • We store event records: title, date and time, location, which email it came from (a message ID), a short quote of up to 100 characters as evidence, and your decision (added, skipped, undone).
  • We store technical logs containing message IDs and at most the first 30 characters of an email subject. Logs never contain full email text, passwords or access tokens.
  • Access tokens for your Google account are stored encrypted and are used only to perform the actions above.

3. Who processes your data

ServiceWhat it does
Anthropic (Claude API)Reads tracked emails to find and verify events. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models.
Amazon Web Services (Taipei region)Stores event records and encrypted tokens, and runs the bot.
TelegramDelivers messages between you and the bot.
GoogleProvides Gmail, Calendar and Drive.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. No person reads your emails, except when you ask for help with a specific problem and give permission, or when required by law.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Schedule Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features described here, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Event records are kept while you use Schedule Bot, so it can avoid adding the same event twice.
  • To delete all your data, send /delete to the bot. It asks you to confirm, then deletes your data right away.
  • If you can no longer use the bot, email nanoclawmi@gmail.com and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • You can remove Schedule Bot's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already added to your calendar stay there until you delete them.

6. Children

Schedule Bot is for adults, such as parents managing family schedules. It is not intended for use by children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and tell current users through the bot.
